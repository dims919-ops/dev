# Interview Guide V1: GenAI in Legal Work

## Research purpose

The purpose of these interviews is to understand how legal professionals perceive the use of Generative AI in legal and institutional work, especially in relation to work processes, productivity, risk, decision-making, and professional roles.

## Opening question

1. Can you briefly describe your current legal work and the main types of tasks you perform during a normal working week?

## Section 1: Legal work processes

2. Which legal tasks take the most time in your weekly work?
3. Which tasks are repetitive, document-heavy, or administrative?
4. Which tasks require the most legal judgment?
5. Which tasks involve coordination with other institutions, departments, clients, courts, or external professionals?

## Section 2: GenAI usefulness

6. Have you used any GenAI tools, such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, or similar tools, in your work?
7. For which tasks do you think GenAI could realistically help legal professionals?
8. Where could GenAI save time without reducing the quality of legal work?
9. Could GenAI help with summarising, drafting, translation, document review, legal research, or preparing internal notes?

## Section 3: Risk and limitations

10. In which legal tasks would GenAI be risky or inappropriate?
11. What are your main concerns about using GenAI in legal work?
12. How important are confidentiality, data protection, and professional responsibility in this context?
13. Would you trust GenAI output without human verification?

## Section 4: Organisational decision-making

14. Who should decide whether GenAI may be used in legal work?
15. Should GenAI use be decided by individual legal professionals, managers, IT departments, or institutional leadership?
16. What kind of internal rules or guidelines would be needed?
17. Should institutions provide official tools instead of allowing employees to use public AI tools?

## Section 5: Professional roles

18. Do you think GenAI will change the role of lawyers or legal officers?
19. Which skills will become more important because of GenAI?
20. Do you think GenAI will reduce legal work, improve legal work, or mainly change how legal work is organised?

## Closing question

21. If your organisation introduced GenAI tomorrow, what would be the first safe and useful legal task where it could be tested?
